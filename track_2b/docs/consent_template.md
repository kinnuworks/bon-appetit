# Receipt donation: consent form (template)

Bon·Appétit's evaluation uses real product names with synthetic receipt lines. To test on real receipts, we ask households to donate them under this form.

**What we ask for.** Photos or exports of supermarket receipts from the last weeks.

**What we do with them.** We remove card numbers, loyalty numbers, store addresses, dates and times, and anything else that points to you, before anyone else sees them. We keep only the article lines, pack sizes and prices.

**AI use.** The article lines are used to test and improve how the open model Apertus reads receipts. They may be published as an open dataset under the CDLA-Permissive-2.0 licence, and others may use that dataset to evaluate or train AI models. Tick one:

- [ ] Yes, my anonymised article lines may be published and used for AI evaluation and training.
- [ ] Only for evaluation inside this project, not published, not used for training.

**Withdrawal.** You can withdraw at any time before publication by writing to the project. After publication we remove your lines from the next version.
